GB210 · AI-Enabled Business Solutions Lab

Students learn to build (“vibecode”) digital solutions for business clients using AI tools, and learn a general framework for scoping and solving business problems. The course takes a learning-by-doing approach — drills, simulated client engagements, and real builds — to prepare students for real-world client projects.

Course components

Vibecoding for Business School Students Course textbook on building digital solutions with AI agents. ↗ The SOLVE Framework Course textbook on scoping and solving business problems — framework, drills, and reference cards. ↗ Wisconsin Case Lab Simulated client engagements. Each exercise requires an access code. ↗ Sample Course Schedule Week-by-week plan with links to all readings, tutorials, and assignments. →
GB210 is a seven-week course, offered for the first time in Spring 2027 — in both the first-half and second-half parts of the semester. Each offering meets MWF, with Fridays devoted to AI tutorials and weekly assignments due Sunday at 11:59 PM. Each offering runs out of its own Canvas site, where assignments, submissions, grades, and announcements live. This site is the course’s companion: links to course materials, for students and anyone else who wants to reference them.

The course is being developed by Xiaoyang Long (xiaoyang.long@wisc.edu) and Jordan Tong (jordan.tong@wisc.edu).
